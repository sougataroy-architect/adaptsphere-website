# AdaptSphere positioning

## What we do
We examine a company's existing workflows, applications, and data, identify where AI can improve daily work, and build and integrate the selected solution. Delivery includes evaluation, deployment, and operational handover.

## Differentiator
Governed AI forward-deployed engineering: useful implementation with explicit authority, accountable ownership, data boundaries, human approvals, verification evidence, and lifecycle controls. Governance research informs the delivery method. Do not identify or link a founder, personal research website, newsletter, book, or publication on the public website.

## Audience and outcome
Business, IT, security, and operations leaders who need help applying AI to existing operations. Lead with practical work: reduce repetitive tasks, help staff find information, connect applications, and support decisions. Treat these as use cases to assess, not guaranteed results.

## Platform scope
Microsoft is the primary specialization, not the boundary of the practice. Assess the workload, current systems, constraints, and platform fit before prescribing a product.

## Ten-second homepage check
A visitor should be able to explain: (1) we review their existing systems, (2) identify useful AI opportunities, (3) build and integrate solutions, (4) define access, approvals, and ownership throughout. Explain the service before using FDE terminology. Primary action: discuss their workflow.

## Writing and proof
Use concrete actions and plain language. Preserve the existing design and seven service routes. Never invent client logos, performance metrics, customer results, certifications, or compliance guarantees. Retain the larger responsive typography. Do not change mail or DNS during content work.
